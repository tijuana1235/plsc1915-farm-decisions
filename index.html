Know what the numbers mean

Revenue, return, and ROI

Revenue

Gold received from selling crops or wine. Sales are not profit: seeds and equipment also use resources.

Net return

Receipts minus the modeled cash costs and the sale value of materials or Ancient Seeds used. Unpaid sales, growing plants, and remaining equipment are listed separately or left unvalued.

ROI

Return on investment compares the added net return from your plan with the new investment's gold and material value. It is a percentage for the selected period, not an annual rate.

Recovery through the cutoff

The first day your cumulative net return catches up with the baseline and stays at or above it through the selected cutoff. A temporary lead is not recovery. Extending the period can change this answer.

ROI = (plan net return − baseline net return) ÷ new investment value × 100

A zero investment has no defined ROI. Qi Gems are shown separately; a gold-based ROI does not repay or value them. Time saved is reported as time, not converted to invented wages.

Inspect the model

Equations, game rules, and classroom assumptions

The game determines crop growth, sale prices, equipment coverage, and upgrade costs. A published game formula does not establish an exact farm work rate. Our walking and action timings are fixed classroom assumptions, so capacity and payback are conditional estimates.
